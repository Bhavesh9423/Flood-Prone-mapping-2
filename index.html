📐 GIS Spatial Analysis Methodology

Flood vulnerability across Indian districts is determined using a Weighted Overlay Analysis combining four core environmental and geographic parameters processed via GIS.

35%

Annual Rainfall

Precipitation volume. Higher rainfall directly compounds surface runoff and waterlogging.

30%

Elevation (DEM)

Digital Elevation Model. Low-lying coastal and basin regions (<50m) face severe accumulation.

25%

River Proximity

Distance from major river channels (Ganga, Brahmaputra, Krishna, Tapi, Adyar).

10%

Land Use & Urbanization

Impervious surface coverage and dense population centers reducing soil infiltration.

MODEL FORMULA:
Risk Score = (Rainfall × 0.35) + (Elevation × 0.30) + (River × 0.25) + (LandUse × 0.10)
Score 3.5 – 5.0 High Risk: Coastal delta, low elevation <50m, heavy rainfall >2000mm.
Score 2.0 – 3.4 Medium Risk: Moderate plains (50–200m), rainfall 1000–2000mm.
Score 1.0 – 1.9 Low Risk: Elevated plateau (>200m), rainfall <1000mm.

📋 Immediate Flood Response Checklist:

  • ✅ Move family and essential valuables to designated high ground / shelters immediately.
  • ✅ Disconnect main electricity breaker and gas valves before leaving.
  • ✅ Keep an emergency waterproof kit with drinking water, dry food, flashlight, and medications.
  • ⚠️ Never attempt to walk, swim, or drive through moving flood waters.
